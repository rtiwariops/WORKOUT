1 Month Workout Plan

Achieve a healthy and aesthetic physique with reduced body fat, improved cardiovascular health, power, endurance and strength.

Plan Overview

  • 4 Days Strength Training + 2 Days Cardio + 1 Day Active Rest
  • 3 sets per exercise, reps: 15, 12, 10 (or 12, 10, 8)
  • Always train with proper muscle-mind connection — this is the fastest way to build muscle
  • Start every exercise with light weight for blood flow and focus

Warm-Up Routine (5-10 min before every session)

  • Neck, shoulder, hand and lower back rotations
  • Front and side leg kicks
  • Shadow boxing
  • Light jump or walk for 2-3 minutes
  • Dynamic stretching of all body parts for 2 minutes
  • Back bending and forward bending

Day 7 — Active Rest Day

Light walking, stretching, mobility work. Stay active but let your muscles recover.

Post-Workout Stretching (Mandatory after every session)

  • Forward bending & wide leg spread cross-leg touch
  • Back bending (Chakrasana / Bridge or Half Bridge)
  • Shoulder stretch, neck stretch, triceps stretch
  • Hamstring and quadriceps stretch
  • Hanging (dead hang from bar)

Things to Remember

  • Diet is 80%, workout is 20%. Training is the stimulant — your results come from nutrition and lifestyle.
  • Stay active throughout the day. Use stairs, walk for distances up to 1 km instead of driving.
  • Deep breathing & meditation — at least 5 minutes daily to manage stress and cortisol. This can increase your gains up to 40%.
  • Static stretching after every workout is non-negotiable. It prevents injuries and increases strength.
  • Meet friends and play with family. Social connection supports recovery and well-being.
